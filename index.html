About MOCHI

MOCHI is a premium 3D endless runner that blends cinematic visuals with tight, responsive gameplay. Inspired by the golden age of arcade runners and modern visual design, MOCHI delivers a polished experience that runs directly in your browser.

Play as Mochi, a curious explorer navigating vibrant valleys, avoiding obstacles, collecting shards, and mastering abilities that change how you play. With a deep progression system, dynamic worlds, and expressive character animations, every run feels new.

3D Character

Full-body 3D model with 6 expressions and 7 animations, rendered with cinematic lighting and glowing core.

Valley Worlds

42 ancient trees, dynamic crystal clusters, floating islands, and 14 recycling trail segments across multiple biomes.

the Game

Three-Lane Runner

Switch lanes left/right to dodge obstacles, jump over boulders and crystal clusters, or slide under red wisps. Keyboard, mouse, and touch supported.

Multiplier System

Build x1→x8 multiplier by picking up coins and fragments. Momentum matters - higher speed = higher rewards. Miss and reset.

Collectibles

Gold coins (+10), blue fragment shards (+25), and rare artifacts (+score multiplier). Each run randomizes the layout.

Abilities

Unlock core shield, coin magnet, and feather fall. Each ability costs fragments and has levels, equippable before each run.

Features

Cinematic Lighting

Volumetric fog, god rays, day→dusk transitions, and dynamic biome lighting that changes how the world looks and feels.

Polished Animations

Frame-perfect character animations: idle, run, jump, slide, collect, rare, hit, and victory. Expressions change based on gameplay moments.

Progression System

XP & leveling, titles, daily challenges, local leaderboard, and airdrop eligibility based on distance, artifacts, and level.

Worlds & Biomes

Mosswood Trail, Crystal Hollow, and Skyfall Ridge - each with unique visual styles, obstacle patterns, and speed characteristics.
